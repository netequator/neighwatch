# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for all contributors, maintainers, and community members.

We value technical rigor, constructive discourse, and collaborative engineering. We expect all participants to conduct themselves with professionalism, integrity, and mutual respect.

---

## Standards of Conduct

### Expected Behavior
- **Technical Rigor:** Base discussions, design debates, and code reviews on technical evidence, RFC specifications, test results, and reproducible data.
- **Respectful Discourse:** Treat all contributors with courtesy, patience, and professional consideration regardless of background or experience level.
- **Constructive Critique:** Focus critique on code, protocols, architecture, and design tradeoffs rather than individuals.
- **Collaboration:** Foster an environment where security researchers, network engineers, and developers can openly collaborate and report issues safely.

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Personal attacks, derogatory comments, or insulting language.
- Harassment, trolling, or disruptive conduct in issue trackers, pull requests, or project discussions.
- Publishing private information (such as personal email addresses or physical locations) without explicit permission.
- Promotion of malicious activities, weaponized exploits, or uncoordinated zero-day drops against live infrastructure.

---

## Enforcement & Responsibilities

The project maintainers (`netequator`) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct:
1. Contact the maintainers privately at `netequator@users.noreply.github.com`.
2. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
3. Violations may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent exclusion from project repositories and communication channels.
